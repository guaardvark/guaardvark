name or the logo. This page says how the marks may be used without asking.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, service, plugin, extension, article or event works with, runs on, or
  is built for Guaardvark: "for Guaardvark", "Guaardvark-compatible", "runs on Guaardvark",
  "Powered by Guaardvark".
- Use the name to refer to the project truthfully: in reviews, tutorials, talks, books, blog
  posts, package descriptions and issue trackers.
- Use the unmodified logo alongside such references, at a reasonable size, without altering
  its colours or proportions.
- Redistribute unmodified official releases under the name, with the notices intact.

## Please ask first

- Using the name or logo in the name of a product, service, company, domain name, app-store
  listing or social-media handle ("Guaardvark Hosting", "guaardvark-cloud.example", "@guaardvark_pro").
- Selling hosting, support or training under the name in a way that suggests it is official.
- Using the logo on merchandise.

Write to the address in the repository README. Answers are quick, and most reasonable uses are
approved.

## Not allowed

- Presenting a modified version, fork, or derived product as Guaardvark, or with branding that
  could be confused with it. Forks are welcome under the MIT License; give the fork its own name.
- Suggesting endorsement by, affiliation with, or origin from Albenze, Inc. where none exists.
- Registering or applying to register any mark or domain containing "Guaardvark" or a confusingly
  similar spelling.
- Altering the logo or combining it with other marks.

## Why this exists

An open-source license lets anyone use the code. A trademark lets users trust that something
called Guaardvark comes from the project. Keeping the two separate protects both.

Questions or requests: open an issue titled "Trademark" in the repository, or use the contact
address in the README.
